Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much sunscreen do I actually need for full face coverage?

Follow the two-finger rule: squeeze two full lines of sunscreen along the length of your pointer and middle fingers (roughly 1/4 teaspoon). This guarantees the laboratory-tested SPF 50 rating on your skin.
