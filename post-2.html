When Meal Kits Win Hands Down

Meal kits aren't always cheaper in raw dollars, but they consistently win in three real-world scenarios:

  • Small Households (1-2 people): You never have to buy a full jar of hoisin sauce or a whole bunch of cilantro just to use half a teaspoon.
  • Takeout Replacement: If a meal kit prevents you from ordering $35 Uber Eats on an exhausted Wednesday evening, it saves you money immediately.
  • Variety & Skill: You learn restaurant-grade recipes without hours of meal planning.
