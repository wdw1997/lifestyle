1. Spotting Oxidation: How to Avoid Spoiled Serums

L-Ascorbic acid is notoriously unstable in the presence of oxygen and light. Fresh active serum should range from crystal clear to pale champagne. If your serum turns deep amber or brown, it has oxidized and lost efficacy. Always store Vitamin C bottles in a dark medicine cabinet or a skincare fridge.
