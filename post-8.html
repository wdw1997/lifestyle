Drugstore Dupes: 4 Affordable Skincare Swaps That Beat Luxury Brands

By Dongwei Wang · Published October 25, 2026

But when cosmetic chemists review product formulations under the microscope, active ingredients tell a very different story: Hyaluronic acid, ceramides, and niacinamide perform identically whether they come in a $12 bottle or a $95 jar.

"You are rarely paying for superior active ingredients in $80+ face serums; you are paying for the brand's Fifth Avenue flagship rent and celebrity endorsement budgets."
