Boredom Busters: 4 Interactive Puzzle Toys for Indoor Cats

By Dongwei Wang · Feline Enrichment Guide

Indoor cats sleep up to 16 hours a day, but lack of environmental stimulation can lead to nocturnal hyperactivity, aggressive scratching on doorframes, and stress-induced obesity. Cats are predatory hunters designed to forage for small rewards.

"Transitioning your cat from bowl-feeding to slow puzzle foraging taps into primal hunting instincts, reducing anxiety and preventing digestive vomiting."

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I teach a cat to use a puzzle feeder?

Start on the easiest setting by placing high-value freeze-dried chicken treats visibly on top of the open compartments. Once they understand paws unlock treats, increase difficulty.

Final Thought

A mentally stimulated cat is a peaceful, well-behaved housemate. Adding 15 minutes of interactive foraging restores balance to indoor feline life.
