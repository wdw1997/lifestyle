The Portable Changing Caddy: How to Skip the Bulky Changing Table

By Dongwei Wang · Minimalist Nursery Guide

Dedicated wooden changing tables consume massive floor space in smaller apartments and homes. Worse yet, seasoned parents quickly realize that walking upstairs to a nursery eight times a day for diaper changes is completely impractical.

"Most diaper changes happen wherever you are: on the living room rug, the master bed, or inside the car trunk. A portable diaper caddy replaces a $250 piece of furniture."

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What should every diaper changing station include?

Keep it simple: fragrance-free water wipes, zinc oxide barrier cream, disposable or wipeable changing pads, hand sanitizer, and two spare cotton bodysuits.

Final Nursery Strategy

Skip single-purpose furniture that babies outgrow within a year. A portable caddy and a wipeable waterproof mat give you total flexibility at a fraction of the cost.
